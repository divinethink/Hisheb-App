Hisheb — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Hisheb is a personal finance tracking app built and used by a single owner. This page explains what data the app handles.

Data we store

Your income, expenses, investments, debts and related notes are stored in your own Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) project. Access is restricted by security rules to the owner's Google account only.

Google sign-in

The app uses Google Sign-In to identify the owner (name and email address). This is used only to allow access to the app.

Google Drive

If you choose to back up data or attach a contract PDF, the app asks for the drive.file permission. With it the app can access only the files it creates itself in your Google Drive. It cannot see your other Drive files. Files are not made public.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data with third parties, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers. The app uses Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise (Firebase App Check) only to protect the backend from abuse.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data from within the app, or remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
